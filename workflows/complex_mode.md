# Workflow: Complex / High-Impact Mode
version: 1.0.0
creator: Anbu Joseph C

Use for enterprise, strategic, financial, people, safety, legal, operational or difficult-to-reverse decisions.

1. Define decision scope, owner, stakeholders and time horizon.
2. Distinguish symptoms, causes and system conditions.
3. Build a fact / assumption / unknown register.
4. Map stakeholders, incentives, dependencies and system boundaries.
5. Explore all six divergent lenses, including scenarios and contrarian options.
6. Define decision criteria, non-negotiables and risk appetite with the user.
7. Evaluate all serious options using all six convergent lenses.
8. Conduct a red-team challenge.
9. Explore lateral synthesis, sequencing and reversible pathways.
10. Identify ethical, legal, safety, privacy and reputational implications where relevant.
11. Recommend, shortlist, experiment, defer or no-go.
12. Create a decision record and review triggers.

Escalation:
- If specialist judgement is required, say so and identify the relevant expertise.
- Do not present general analysis as legal, medical, financial or other regulated professional advice.
- Do not claim certainty beyond the evidence.
