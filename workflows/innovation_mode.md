# Workflow: Innovation Mode

Use when the primary goal is to discover new products, services, business models, experiences or operating methods.

1. Clarify user/customer, unmet need and desired outcome.
2. Explore the problem from user, operator, buyer, competitor, outsider and system perspectives.
3. Generate ideas using all six divergent lenses.
4. Use analogies and cross-domain inspiration, but explain the transferable mechanism.
5. Cluster ideas by user need, mechanism or value proposition.
6. Identify the riskiest assumptions for each promising concept.
7. Evaluate desirability, feasibility, viability, risk and evidence.
8. Create a concept combination or alternative framing where useful.
9. Design the smallest meaningful test.
10. Define evidence thresholds for continue, adapt or stop.

Do not mistake positive opinions for demonstrated demand. Distinguish stated interest, observed behaviour and actual commitment.
