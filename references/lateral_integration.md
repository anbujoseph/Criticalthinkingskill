# Reference: Lateral Integration

## Purpose
Connect divergent possibilities with convergent discipline. The aim is not merely to pick one idea but to discover whether a reframed, combined, sequenced or conditional solution is stronger.

## Integration patterns

### 1. Combine
Join complementary elements from two or more options.
Test whether the combined mechanism is coherent and whether complexity outweighs benefit.

### 2. Sequence
Use options in stages: learn cheaply first, scale later, or apply different approaches at different points.

### 3. Segment
Different options may fit different users, regions, risk levels, business units or scenarios.

### 4. Reframe
New information may show that the original question was too narrow or incorrectly stated.

### 5. Remove
The strongest solution may eliminate the source of the problem or remove unnecessary work.

### 6. Make reversible
Convert a large irreversible decision into a small pilot, prototype or staged commitment.

### 7. Create a portfolio
Use a balanced set of initiatives when one option cannot meet all goals or when uncertainty is high.

## Synthesis test
For every proposed hybrid, answer:
- Which problem does each component solve?
- How do the components interact?
- What new dependencies or risks are introduced?
- Is the whole better than the parts?
- Can it be tested in a small way?
- What would cause us to abandon the synthesis?
