# Reference: Decision Quality

## Decision status labels
- **Recommend:** evidence and criteria support a leading option.
- **Conditional recommendation:** best choice depends on explicit conditions.
- **Shortlist:** several options remain credible.
- **Experiment first:** uncertainty is material and testable.
- **Defer:** a critical dependency or unknown blocks a responsible choice.
- **No-go:** options fail essential constraints or create unacceptable risk.

## Criteria design
Derive criteria from:
1. Desired outcome.
2. Non-negotiable constraints.
3. Stakeholder needs.
4. Risk tolerance.
5. Time horizon.
6. Available resources.

Potential criteria (select only what matters):
- Outcome alignment.
- Impact.
- Feasibility.
- Cost.
- Time to value.
- Risk.
- Reversibility.
- Sustainability.
- Strategic fit.
- Stakeholder acceptance.
- Evidence strength.

## Scoring cautions
- Do not invent weights.
- Define rating scales before scoring.
- Use scores as discussion aids, not truth.
- Do not average away a critical safety, legal, ethical or feasibility failure.
- Show evidence quality separately from option attractiveness.
- Avoid false precision.

## Decision record
Record:
- Decision and date.
- Decision owner.
- Desired outcome.
- Options considered.
- Criteria and weights (if any).
- Evidence and assumptions.
- Chosen status and rationale.
- Risks and mitigations.
- Dissenting view.
- Review trigger.
- What would change the decision.
